Distance & Toll Calculator

Know how far it is and exactly how much toll you will pay.

Distance is the difference between the two kilometre marks on the same corridor. Toll for non-car vehicles uses demo multipliers. Real rates differ, so check the official rate at the plaza.

Is the annual pass worth it?

Compare paying toll on every trip with buying a yearly pass. Calculate a trip above and the first two boxes fill in automatically.

The pass price and crossing limit are set at the top of js/data.js. Annual passes are meant for private non-commercial vehicles. Confirm the current price and rules on the official NHAI website.
