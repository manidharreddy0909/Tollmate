How to check your real balance

This page only compares the number you enter with the trip toll. To see your actual balance:

📱

Issuer app

Open the mobile app or website of the bank or company that issued your FASTag.

💬

SMS alerts

Your issuer usually sends a message after every toll deduction showing the balance.

📞

Helpline

Call the customer care number printed on your tag, or the NHAI helpline 1033.

Tip: keep a small extra amount in your tag so a slightly higher toll does not block your vehicle at the gate.
